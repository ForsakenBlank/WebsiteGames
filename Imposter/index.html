How to play

  1. Pass the phone around. Everyone secretly looks at their card.
  2. The crew all get the same word. The imposter only knows they're the imposter.
  3. Go round the circle, each person gives one clue about the word.
  4. Vote for who you think is faking. Crew get a point each if they catch the imposter.
  5. An imposter who isn't caught scores 2. A caught imposter can still steal 1 point by guessing the word.
