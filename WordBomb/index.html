How to play

  1. Sit in a circle. Whoever holds the phone says a word that fits the letters, out loud.
  2. Everyone agrees it counts? Tap the button and pass the phone to the left.
  3. The fuse is secret. It can blow after a few seconds or much later.
  4. Whoever holds it when it blows loses a life. Last one standing wins.
  5. No repeating words in the same round!
