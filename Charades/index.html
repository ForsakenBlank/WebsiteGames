How to play

  1. Pick a deck and hold the phone up to your forehead, screen facing your friends.
  2. They act it out, sing it or describe it without saying the word.
  3. Got it? Tilt the phone down or tap the right side. Want to pass? Tilt up or tap the left side.
  4. Play in two teams and take turns to see who gets the most.
