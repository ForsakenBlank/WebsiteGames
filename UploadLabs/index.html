Upload Labs

Build a data business from a single terminal. Download files, process them into something valuable, and upload them for cash.

  • Start by hand: press Download and Upload on your Terminal. Buy a Downloader and an Uploader to automate it.
  • Drag from an output port (right) to another program to connect a wire. Click a wire to cut it. Drag programs by their title bar, drag the background to pan, scroll to zoom.
  • Files have sizes. Downloaders and uploaders move MB/s, so a video takes much longer than a text file. Processed files are worth far more per MB.
  • Some downloads are infected 🦠, uploading them gets you fined. Use a Sorter or Antivirus.
  • A wire jams when the program at the end can't use the file at the front. Sort files with a Sorter, or send leftovers to the Trash.
  • Every program uses power ⚡. Go over capacity and everything slows down, build Generators.
  • Hackers attack once you start earning. Research Network security for Firewalls, or hammer Counter-hack before the timer runs out.
  • Blueprints: hold Shift and drag (or use ⬚ Select) to pick programs, save them, then paste the whole setup from the 📐 tab.
  • Challenges (🏆 tab) are timed scenarios with their own lab, clearing one earns shares for your main company.
