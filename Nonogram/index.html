How to play

  • The numbers say how many filled squares are in each run, in order, along that row or column.
  • Runs are separated by at least one empty square.
  • Fill squares you're sure about. Mark ones you know are empty with ×.
  • Every puzzle can be solved with logic, no guessing needed.
  • Drag to paint lots of squares at once. Wrong fills cost a heart.
